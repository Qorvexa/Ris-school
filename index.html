About RIS

Where World-Class Education Meets Values

RIS International Schools provide a modern international education built around academic achievement, strong character and a genuine respect for every student's potential.

From kindergarten through secondary school, students learn in a supportive environment where curiosity, creativity, leadership and global awareness are encouraged.
